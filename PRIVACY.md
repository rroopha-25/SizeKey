# Privacy Policy

**SizeKey**
Effective date: November 4, 2026

SizeKey helps you find clothing, innerwear, footwear and diaper sizes from your body measurements. It is built so that your measurements stay with you.

## What we collect

Nothing. SizeKey has no accounts, no sign-up, no server database and no analytics or advertising trackers.

## What stays on your device

When you type measurements or save a family member's profile, that information is stored only in your own browser's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. This includes:

- Height, weight and body measurements
- Names you give to saved profiles
- Your settings (country, units, filters)

You can delete it at any time by clearing the measurements on the page, removing saved people, or clearing your browser's site data.

## Children's information

Parents and guardians can enter measurements for children and babies. That information is handled the same way as everything else: it stays in the browser on the device where it was entered and is never collected by SizeKey.

## Third-party services

Loading the page involves two outside services, which may see your IP address and basic browser details as part of normal web traffic:

- **Website hosting.** The site's host serves the page files and may keep standard server logs.
- **Google Fonts.** The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Neither service receives your measurements.

## Brand links and sizes

SizeKey shows estimated sizes for brands. It does not share any information with those brands, and we are not affiliated with them.

## Cookies

SizeKey does not set cookies.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
