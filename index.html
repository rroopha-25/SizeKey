How to measure
  • Use a soft tailor's tape. No tape? Use a string, then lay it against a ruler.
  • Keep the tape level and snug, not tight. Measure over light clothing.
  • For foot length, stand on paper against a wall and mark the tip of your longest toe. Measure both feet and use the longer one.
  • For babies, measure lying down, from the top of the head to the heel.
